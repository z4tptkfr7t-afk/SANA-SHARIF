01 / PROFILE

About me.

I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Lakehead University, working on resource-efficient communication and networking for 6G and beyond.

My work focuses on UAV-assisted IoT networks, semantic communication, resource optimization, and emerging architectures for intelligent wireless systems. I have contributed to peer-reviewed journal and conference publications, and I am engaged in teaching, mentorship, and academic leadership.

Education
